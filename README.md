# Chemineers Society, IIT Kanpur

**The student body of the Department of Chemical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.**

Official website: **https://chemineers.com/**

---

## About the Society

The Chemineers Society is a student body that promotes intellectual, academic and cultural activities among the students of the Chemical Engineering department at IIT Kanpur. It supports their overall development and keeps students, faculty and alumni connected.

The society works to:

- Help students identify and make use of campus resources.
- Build strong relationships among the students, faculty and alumni of the department.
- Make sure there is no communication gap between the people of the department.

## What We Do

Throughout the year the society organizes events for the whole department community.

### Exergy
**Exergy** is the pan-India Chemical Engineering fest of IIT Kanpur, first held in 2023. It brings together talks, workshops, poster presentations and competitions, with speakers drawn from renowned chemical engineers. The name reflects the idea of realizing our full potential as Chemical Engineering students.
Visit the festival site: https://chemineerssocietyiitk.github.io/Exergy2024/

### Simutech
Simutech is the society's technical wing. It runs workshops on software such as MATLAB, Python, Aspen and COMSOL, and offers mentor-led core and non-core projects that prepare students for the internship season. Past topics include process simulation, optimization, multiphase microfluidics, deep learning in Chemical Engineering and Monte Carlo simulations.

### Community
- **Freshers and Farewell:** a warm welcome for new students and a proper goodbye to graduating ones.
- **Happy Hours:** informal gatherings with tea and snacks that bring students and professors closer.
- **Games and Sports:** including the Chemineers Phatta League and exhibition matches between professors and students.

### Chem-E-Blogs
A biweekly blog series on recent happenings and rapid advances in Chemical Engineering, covering topics such as semiconductors, process intensification and AI in education.
